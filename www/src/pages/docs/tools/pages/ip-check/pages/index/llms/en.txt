# My IP Address

An interactive tool that shows your connection the way the internet sees it — the data a website can read from your IP address:

- **Public IP** — your IPv4 and IPv6 address
- **Location** — the geographic location derived from your IP
- **ISP** — your internet service provider
- **ASN** — the autonomous system number of your network

Use it to confirm your own address, or — once connected to Phantom-WG — to verify that the tunnel's exit IP is shown instead of your real IP.
